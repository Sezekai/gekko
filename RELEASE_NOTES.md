Gekko 1.2.1: fixes 1.2.0 not starting.

- 1.2.0 stopped at start with "Something went wrong: 'accent'" when your library had songs with matching file names (the Duplicates tab used a colour name the theme doesn't have). Fixed, and the same mistake in the Tidy up filters too.
- Duplicates are now worked out when you open the tab, not while Gekko starts.
- The tests now fail on any error inside the app's screens, and check every colour name, so this kind of mistake can't slip through again.

Everything from 1.2.0 (the reworked Tidy up, the duplicate finder, lists that remember their columns and sizes) is in this version.

Replace the App folder in E:\Pictures\Gekko with the one in Gekko.zip.
