Gekko 1.1.0: listening history.

- New History screen: every song you play, in order and when. Repeats are listed each time (and marked when they're in a row), skips are shown greyed.
- Group it by day, week, month or year, as a list or a cover grid.
- Pick any span of days (today, this week, last 30 days… or two dates on a calendar). Play what you listened to then, or open the songs you didn't.
- Each song has its own history: right-click > Listening history (or I), and a History tab in the full-screen player.
- New filters: listened to / not listened to in a span of days, and play counts from-to. Smart playlists can use them ("not played in the last 30 days").
- Song lists have a Last played column. Settings > Reset can clear the history.

History starts with this version: songs you played before still have their play counts, but no dated entries.

Replace the App folder in E:\Pictures\Gekko with the one in Gekko.zip.
